# Acme Cloud Handbook

## Refunds
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, refunds are issued as account credit only. Refund requests are handled by the billing team within 3 business days.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm IST. Priority customers get a response within 2 hours; standard customers within one business day.

## Data retention
Uploaded documents are stored encrypted. Deleted documents are permanently removed from backups after 30 days.

## Shipping
Orders placed before 2pm IST ship the same business day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within India and 7 to 12 days internationally. Tracking links are emailed once the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Security
All accounts support two-factor authentication through an authenticator app. Administrators can require two-factor for the whole workspace. API keys can be rotated at any time and old keys stop working immediately.

## Billing
Invoices are generated on the first of each month. Annual plans receive a 15 percent discount. Payment methods include cards and UPI for Indian customers.
