# Nimbus Notes FAQ

## What is Nimbus Notes?
Nimbus Notes is a note-taking app for teams. It supports markdown, shared notebooks and offline editing on desktop and mobile.

## How do I export my notes?
Open Settings, choose Data, and click Export. You can export a single notebook as Markdown or PDF, or every notebook as a ZIP archive. Large exports are emailed as a download link that expires after 48 hours.

## Can I use Nimbus Notes offline?
Yes. Notebooks you mark as Available Offline sync automatically when you reconnect. Conflicting edits are kept as separate versions so nothing is silently overwritten.

## What are the storage limits?
The Free plan includes 2 GB of storage and 5 notebooks. The Team plan includes 100 GB per workspace and unlimited notebooks. Individual attachments cannot exceed 250 MB.

## How do I recover a deleted note?
Deleted notes stay in Trash for 30 days. Open Trash, select the note and choose Restore. After 30 days the note is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by support.

## Do you offer student discounts?
Verified students get the Team plan at 50 percent off. Verification uses a university email address and must be renewed every year.
