# Security Policy

## Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not appear in known breach lists. Password managers are recommended. Passwords expire only when a compromise is suspected.

## Incident response
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery. The on-call engineer opens an incident channel, assigns an incident commander and records a timeline. A written post-mortem is published within five business days.

## Access reviews
Administrative access is reviewed every quarter. Accounts belonging to departed employees are disabled on their last working day. Contractors receive time-limited access that expires automatically after 90 days.

## Encryption
Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Encryption keys are rotated every year and stored in a managed key vault.

## Vulnerability disclosure
Researchers can report vulnerabilities to the security mailbox. We acknowledge reports within two business days and aim to fix critical issues within 14 days. We do not pursue legal action against good-faith research.
