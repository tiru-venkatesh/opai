FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it paid?

Not at the moment. Internships are unpaid. If a stipend or performance-based reward is added, it will be confirmed in writing before you start.

Is it remote?

Yes. Weekly check-ins are scheduled at a time that works in India Standard Time.

Do I need prior experience?

Not for every role. We look for curiosity and proof that you learn by building, such as a small project or repository.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, once you meet the completion criteria agreed at onboarding: weekly check-ins attended and a final demo submitted.

Can this become a full-time role?

Strong interns may be considered for future roles. There is no guarantee.
