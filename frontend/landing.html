how_to_reg

Approval first

OPAI can prepare emails but never sends them automatically. You review the recipient, subject and message first.

mail_lock

Gmail is optional

If you connect it, OPAI requests only gmail.send. It cannot read, search, download or delete your inbox.

shield

Google data

OPAI's use of information from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use. It is not sold, not used for ads, and not used to train AI models.

Questions

Is OPAI a coding judge like LeetCode?

No. OPAI only reminds you to keep your streak; you practice on LeetCode itself.

Can OPAI read my inbox?

No. The optional Gmail permission only sends emails you approved.

Does OPAI send emails by itself?

No. Every draft waits for your approval.

Who is it for?

Students balancing exams, projects and internship applications.

Why is the first load slow sometimes?

The server sleeps when idle and can take up to a minute to wake. After that it is fast.
