TRUST BY DESIGN

Your permissions should make sense.

OPAI explains why it requests access to connected services and how information is used. Users can review our privacy policy to understand the handling of their information and connected-account data.

G

Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In strictly to authenticate your identity and secure your individual workspace session. OAuth requests are limited to fundamental profile attributes (such as your name, email address, and profile photo when configured) necessary to establish your account.

Signing in with Google does NOT automatically authorize reading or accessing your Gmail inbox.
✉

Gmail Permissions

The application requests exclusively the gmail.send scope. This permission enables sending user-approved emails prepared in your Outbox workflow. It does not grant OPAI permission to read, search, index, or download inbox messages.

Scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send (Send only)
AI

AI Processing & Data Handling

User-provided prompts, inquiries, and relevant workspace notes are processed by the configured AI provider (e.g. Groq) to generate assistant responses. Certain outreach workflows may include user-provided professional details (such as name, branch, skills, or CGPA) when explicitly included in prompts.

Data transmission is encrypted in transit and never utilized for unauthorized AI model training.

COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently asked questions.

Clear, verifiable answers regarding OPAI capabilities, privacy, and architecture.

What is OPAI? +

OPAI is an AI-powered workspace and operating agent designed to bring intelligent assistance, project management, academic study roadmaps, and supported productivity tasks together in one focused environment.

What is KARNA? +

KARNA is the AI assistant built inside OPAI. KARNA assists with question exploration, task decomposition, drafting structured email outbox messages, and organizing project roadmaps.

How does Google Sign-In work? +

Google Sign-In provides secure identity authentication. OPAI requests standard user profile information (such as email, name, and profile photo) solely to establish and secure your personal account session.

Does OPAI read my Gmail inbox? +

No. OPAI requests only the gmail.send OAuth scope. This permission does not grant authorization to view, search, index, or download the contents of your inbox.

Can OPAI send emails without my permission? +

Never. Email actions strictly require deliberate user initiation. You review the recipient, subject, and body in the Outbox interface, and dispatch occurs only when you confirm through the approval button.

How is information processed by AI? +

User prompts are dispatched to verified inference infrastructure (such as Groq) to synthesize responses. Data is processed securely over TLS and governed by our privacy agreement and provider terms.

Can I access OPAI on mobile? +

Yes, the public website and workspace are fully responsive, formatted for seamless viewing on handheld mobile displays (390px/360px), tablets, and widescreen desktop monitors.
