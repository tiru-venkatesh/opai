You stay in control

OPAI is in active development. Please don't upload sensitive personal documents yet.

  • Approval first. OPAI can prepare emails but never sends them automatically. You review the recipient, subject and message before approving.
  • Gmail is optional. If you connect it, OPAI requests only gmail.send. It cannot read, search, download or delete your inbox.
  • Google data. OPAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Questions

Is OPAI a coding judge like LeetCode?

No. The DSA Roadmap tells you what to learn next and when to revise. You practice on external sites.

Can OPAI read my inbox?

No. The optional Gmail permission only sends emails you approved.

Does OPAI send emails by itself?

No. Every draft waits for your approval.
