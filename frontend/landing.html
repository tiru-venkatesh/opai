Questions

Before you open it

Where does my data live?

On your device, in the browser, until you connect your own backend. Nothing is sent anywhere by default.

Will it send emails on its own?

No. Every draft sits in the Outbox until you approve it, and sending only happens through your own Gmail sign-in.

Do I need to sign in to try it?

No. Continue as a guest and everything works except sending real email, which needs a Google sign-in.

Is this only for job hunting?

No — it's for anyone running more than one thread at once: applications, outreach, freelance clients and coursework, side by side.
